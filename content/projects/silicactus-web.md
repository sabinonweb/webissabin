The web home of **Silicactus**, built as a modern Next.js application with
TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and pnpm — set up with create-next-app and
deployed Vercel-style from the first commit.

This one sits at the intersection of the engineering and the brand work:
the site isn't just scaffolding, it's the public face of the Silicactus
identity, so typography, spacing, and tone carry as much weight as the
component structure underneath.
