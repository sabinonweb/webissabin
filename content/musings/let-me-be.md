---
title: "Let me be"
date: 2026-02-17
description: "a poem about desire, deception and wanting out"
tags: ["poems"]
back: true
---

Woh, you won't let me be,\
I don't understand your point of desire.\
Or is it a cornerstone that you always deceive me with,\
I don't need answers, just shove my mouth up with a pacifier.

What should I cling to? Hope?\
But it requires everything to be fucked.\
My skull is filled with a pool of shite thoughts,\
Feels like I carry some kind of blocked cerebral aqueduct.

You kick me around a little bit, no, a lot maybe,\
Punch my face every now and then, hit a nerve.\
And the problem isn't not knowing how to dodge,\
The thing is, at some point, I decided that is what I deserve.

You lure me with all those glittering promises,\
No less than a perfectly curated storefront.\
As soon as I enter there, it gets dull,\
The colors fade away, and you are the one to haunt.

You are a liar, a control freak,\
You have taken my space away.\
Desire is yours, to smoke every kind of tobacco out there\
While I lay there, garnering like a ashtray.

Let me go back to the basics.\
Pleaseee play this last game of whac-a-mole.\
Jammed in this twirl, for perpetuity now,\
I never got my own little snack and stroll.

Weirdo, I am safe here, meet me with a glee.\
Untie me already, don't fret for me.\
I have failed miraculously every time, haven't I?\
Tie the knots tight, yet I may plead for them to be loose.\
Let the disciples be silenced and please let me be.
