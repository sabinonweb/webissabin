---
title: "I would rather not say"
date: 2025-04-15
description: "a poem about a life lived waiting for approval"
tags: ["poems"]
back: true
---

I didn't choose this path\
This society injected me\
When I was half inside my mother's womb\
And I hadn't even inhaled a single breath

I was taught to pretend\
Shoving a cloth down my throat\
I had to put my voice down and hold my screams.

Just trying to flower, I tried to roost down a tree's shed\
A wind from 16 blew me off, rather unintentionally\
I haven't been able to find a shed since\
I try and the same society tells me, "You are doing it wrong".

Years passed by….

It was me lying on my deathbed\
Waiting for someone's approval to take my final breath\
Expecting someone to hold my hand while I walked a long path to the cremation ground\
Checking in to see if the person carrying me was smiling.

The clothes were off and I was afraid that someone would judge\
The mantras filled the air and cries played like they were a symphony to my deaf ear\
The river calling me with its waves\
While I was waiting for a hero to take me back home.

The stage was set, and ghee was poured\
Sun ready to burn me and winds prepared to blow me off\
Like I never existed, did I?\
Vultures, excited for fresh blood and soil to get what rightfully is its, back.\
While I was busy counting the number of people that came to see me.

I didn't see you and pleaded fire to hold on a second\
My eyes were desperate and my mind overthinking\
A final goodbye and a sign that you would wait\
But I was too tiny for fire to hear my cries.

My head was off, and my body was burnt\
Air was filled with freshness and rivers smiled again\
Helpless I lay there with a drop tear\
While my heart pounded for my being and love\
Which was always just there.
