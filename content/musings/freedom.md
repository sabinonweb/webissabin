---
title: "The Burden of freedom"
date: 2026-11-07
description: "a poem about the freedom to act and the fear of acting"
tags: ["poems"]
back: true
---

i searched for you\
like a mad dog\
searched for you in every little corner\
got burnt just to get a touch of you

you give me all the power\
a kingmaker\
gloating over my good-ol success\
i was jose mourinho, the special one

feed my burning desire\
like you love me\
and i think you do\
or at least i think so

you take me to those abandoned houses\
i see traces of me there\
who enjoyed every bit of it\
and you said i could fight back

you make me powerless\
given the choices\
and the cost you make me pay\
just getting the touch of you burns me

i owe you a plan\
a lot of explanation\
and you use it against me\
like a mad dog

i found the key when i was in ashes\
with a smell of you\
but it was rather easier to blame myself\
and imagine myself a failure

you take me to those abandoned houses\
i see traces of me there\
who enjoyed every bit of it\
and couldn't fight back
