title: "I wanna blame you"
date: 2026-02-17
description: "a poem about howls, cheers and counting droplets"
tags: ["poems"]

I wanna blame you\
For I think\
You took away my ability\
To think

But as I think\
Did I even have it in the first place?\
Cause had I\
I wouldn’t be blaming you.

But I wanna blame you\
For you can’t speak\
Can’t complain about it.\
But could it be the only reason?\
As you were my resting shoulder\
Until I found someone like me\
Afraid and clueless\

Did I ever hate you?\
At least envied you\
You always had an answer for everything\
Until you didn’t.

Was I too stupid to ask for an answer?\
For the question seemed very idiosyncratic.\
Until, you answered and I didn’t like it.\
How peculiar of me?

You had your own quagmire\
And I had my very own.\
Hallucinations for you,\
Rumination for me.\
The difference?\
You would take a bullet for me\
And I would be happy seeing you fail.

You would do the thinking.\
Thinking for me.\
You and I were same, or were we?\
I had a best friend and you didn’t.

I am disgusted by you\
For you echo back the parts\
Parts I abominated\
Not because of you.

I wanna blame you\
Till you comfort me,\
Heal the wounds,\
Pander me.

But when you blurt out,\
And I am laid bare,\
Naked and unadorned\
With a dawn clear of nemesis\
I am there in the empty courtroom\
Exiled from the rest\
Like a ghost in the open\
A prisoner in the courtroom.
