From my high school english teacher to my perfectionist stereotypical assface uncle, every human being I have come across has only one thing to say, "Everybody's got the same information, everyone is equally equipped with same resources (we'll assume it to be so for this article, fuck it), so what's the difference and what's really the point". If we stop binging on cornflakes for a while and really think about this matter, it is indeed true. There is nothing to set us apart from every existing sapiens, hell even non existent sapiens.

Then there's a need to ask why are we still caught up in the box? There have been people who have stepped out of the box and performed extraordinary feats for the world, then why can't we? Where are we falling short? In the thirst of thinking outside the box, I guess we have forgotten about the box itself. See, you can't think of your mind using your own mind. Can you say what your mind thinks about your mind using your own mind? Stop smiling, yes you can. But the processed information is all the prejudiced stuff and there is nothing new as such.

Meanwhile, as I write this, if I am restricted within a certain boundary let's say, within a topic, then I can think outside the box about it. If there is no confinement within my mind then, I will think about mind, but as I have stated earlier, I can't think about the mind staying within it. But I can think about stars, galaxies and even dog studying physics with glasses on.

There needs to be a box, so it's high time now. We need to stop this shit of trying to change everything and stop for awhile to look at things, with keen observation so that we can really see what the difference really is.
