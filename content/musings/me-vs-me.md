---
title: "Me vs Me"
date: 2026-05-17
description: "a poem about wanting two opposite things at once"
tags: ["poems"]
back: true
---

I dream of gloomy sunshine\
And yet love the serenity of the placid.\
Touch the sky\
And just get melt away in the turf.

I wanna embrace me\
And yet turn into their unsaid expectations.\
Have the courage to learn\
And run away from learning.

I wanna offer a shoulder\
And yet the yearning for a hug screams like a baby.\
Respond with grace\
And judge you for your stupidity.

I wanna be a savvy\
And yet wanna shout the stupidity in me.\
Be whole and solaced\
And carry the wound of my pretense.

And yet I dwelled in duality\
Duality in me\
Tussled back and forth\
Despised mutual reality\
Somehow\
I dwelled in duality\
Duality in me.
