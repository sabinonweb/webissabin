---
title: "Lemme tell you a story"
date: 2025-05-03
description: "a poem that tells a sandstorm instead of a story"
tags: ["poems"]
back: true
---

What do I have?\
A diary and a pen.\
Red one.\
That speaks a thousand stories.\
Stories? Yes, stories.

What could I do about the sandstorm?\
The farther I tried to run away\
The more sucked I was.\
So, I just plugged up my ears and walked into it.

It touched every part of my body.\
Every cell felt the voracity of the storm.\
Should I say, it created bloodshed inside of me, time and again.\
Who did it kill?\
I don't wanna tell you.

Back then, I couldn't have it all.\
Now, I don't want much.\
What changed?\
My appetite? Not as much.\
Did I? Nah, I never had to.

I have my notes filled with wheels.\
Wheels of time? Ehhhh…No.

But still, what if it could take me back in time?\
I would just relive it one more time.\
There isn't a thing I would like to change.\
Not because I don't regret anything.\
But because I regret some of the choices that I made.

What's the story here?\
What's the sandstorm here?\
Sandstorm is a story.\
Only when the red of the diary is in action,\
And not of the bloodshed.
