MoSTI Startup Hackathon grant — what it is and how to apply

Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (विज्ञान, प्रविधि तथा नवप्रवर्तन मन्त्रालय), Singha Durbar. Notice published 2083/06/19 (≈ 5 Oct 2026). Programme activity no. 1.3.5.1, FY 2083/84.

The key point: this notice does not invite teams to compete. It invites registered organisations (आयोजक संस्था) to apply for a government grant to organise a provincial or national startup hackathon. To take part as organisers, Ampixa Labs needs a registered entity with a current renewal. If you only want to compete, wait for the hackathons that the selected organisers will announce.

Deadline

15 days from 2083/06/19, so about 2083/07/04 (≈ 20 Oct 2026). If the 15th day is a public holiday, you can submit during office hours on the next working day. Applications received after the event has already happened are not considered (§7(2)).

You must state which level you are applying for: provincial (प्रदेशस्तर) or national (राष्ट्रियस्तर). Phase 1 is provincial. The national call follows once the provincial hackathons are done (§6).

How to submit

  • Email info@mosti.gov.np, or submit in person at the Ministry.
  • Attach certified documents as listed in §7 / Annex-3 (below).
  • Phone: 01-4200539, 01-4200540

Steps

  1. Confirm you have a registered organisation: a registration certificate plus proof of renewal.
  2. Choose the level (provincial, ≤ Rs 3 lakh, or national, ≤ Rs 13 lakh) and up to 2 thematic areas from the list below.
  3. Prepare the Annex-3 proposal: name, partners, MoU, date and venue, goals, outcomes, participant count, schedule, budget, experts, and the participant selection method.
  4. Add the capability evidence (Annex-3 §4), the self-declaration of no duplicate funding (§14(छ)), and the audit commitment letter.
  5. Email it to info@mosti.gov.np before the deadline.
  6. Shortlisting: the committee evaluates proposals, the shortlist and presentation schedule go up on mosti.gov.np, and you present in person at the Ministry or online (§12).
  7. If approved: you're notified by email, then sign an MoU in the Annex-4 format before running the event.
  8. Run the event. A Ministry representative must sit on the final judging panel.
  9. Within 21 days of finishing, submit the completion report (Annex-5) with bills, photos and invoices. Payment follows, as reimbursement into your bank account.
  10. Get the grant audited and tell the Ministry once that's done.

One organiser can run at most 1 national + 1 provincial, or up to 2 provincial events (§13(7)). Provincial money may go only to event costs and prize money. National money may also cover participants' lodging, training and trainers.

Format of the hackathon (§15)

  • Provincial: a 2-day closed session in all 7 provinces, ideally on the same dates. Each province covers at most 2 thematic areas, with up to 3 winning teams per area. Teams have at most 3 members.
  • National: up to 42 top provincial teams. A 5-day residential training (the organiser handles lodging), then a 3-day national hackathon. The top 5 teams are selected.
  • Top 5: at least 15 days of special training (Idea Strengthening, Business Mentoring, Commercialization). The Annex-6 template splits it into 3 phases: product refinement (days 1–9), business mentoring (10–13), and market entry and pitch, ending in Demo Day (14–15).
  • The Ministry may award certificates of appreciation. The same person can't participate in more than one event.

Thematic areas (Annex-1)

  1. Supply chain management
  2. Disaster risk reduction & early warning systems
  3. Public transportation & mobility
  4. Market intelligence
  5. IT for education system reform
  6. Climate change incl. loss & damage assessment, carbon market intelligence
  7. Industry 4.0 and 5.0
  8. Public infrastructure monitoring
  9. Public infrastructure quality monitoring / civic engagement
  10. Emerging technologies

Documents to attach (Annex-3)

  1. Copy of organisation registration certificate
  2. Proof of up-to-date renewal
  3. Proposed hackathon details:
    • (क) programme name
    • (ख) organiser, collaborating and partner orgs
    • (ग) MoU with partners (if any)
    • (घ) date & venue
    • (ङ) goals & objectives
    • (च) expected outcomes
    • (छ) expected number of participants
    • (ज) schedule
    • (झ) cost estimate
    • (ञ) experts, participating teams and co-organisers
    • (ट) participant selection method
  4. Evidence for evaluation:
    • (क) if you've run one before: its selection details, a transparent and competitive judging process, proof of technical and managerial capacity, and your track record with hackathons, startups or entrepreneurship events
    • (ख) ability to reach many students, researchers, innovators and young entrepreneurs
    • (ग) capacity to collaborate with experts, universities, research institutes and industry
    • (घ) a detailed workplan, timeline, resource mobilisation plan and expected outcomes
    • (ङ) sustainability: a path for solutions into further development, testing, incubation or market
    • (च) ability to give the top teams special training
    • (छ) a commitment letter to submit the audit report copy

Plus a self-declaration that funding from other sources doesn't duplicate this grant, with details of other funding sources (§14(छ),(ज)).

Rules to remember (§14)

  • Don't use the Government of Nepal logo, or call the Ministry a co-organiser, without prior approval.
  • Run the event exactly as proposed in the application.
  • The Ministry's decision on selection is final. Complaints go to a 5-member committee (§23).
  • Selection committee: Joint Secretary (Planning) as convener, 5 domain experts, and the Head of the Technology Promotion & Transfer Section as member-secretary. Evaluation criteria are set by the committee itself.
  • Note: conferences and exhibitions are not being funded this year because of the Bhotekoshi flood disaster (§5). Only hackathons are funded.
