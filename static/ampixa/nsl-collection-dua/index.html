4 One hour of checking

WhyThe timing check on 100 YouTube teaching spans came back 92 of 99 right. Whether the word on each span is right is still unknown, and only a signer can tell.
What we do with itThe signer watches the same 100 clips and answers one question per clip: is this the labelled word?
What it provesIf most are right, the YouTube teaching corpus becomes thousands of trustworthy word clips from a new signer.

5 NTV news data: what we do without the signer

The scrape keeps running in the background. None of these steps needs NSL knowledge.

  1. Extract pose, then delete the video. The models only use body and hand points, and the provenance rules say pose only.
  2. Drop non-lesson videos. In the timing check, 4 of 7 wrong spans came from one intro video. Filter by title.
  3. Timing check on news spans. The same 100-clip check, on the news interpreter. Anyone can tick it.
  4. Count words in captions. Which dictionary words appear in news captions, and how often. This list decides which words to film next.
  5. Mining run. The best model (filmstrip K=2) scans the news pose for strong words and keeps only very confident hits. These go in a queue for the signer to confirm next week. Only confirmed hits join the pile.

Captions are what the anchor says; the interpreter signs the gist. That is why training directly on caption text failed and collapsed to repeating one phrase. The news data becomes useful through confirmed spots, not through its captions.

Schedule

About 90 minutes with the signer per session. Short sessions keep signing natural.

Day 1

Consent form, signer ID. Pilot 10 words, 2 takes each. 49 letters, 3 takes each.

Day 2

15 news words already in the dictionary. 20 new news words.

Day 3

20 sentences. 20 spelled names.

Day 4

One hour: word check on the 100 YouTube spans. Re-take anything that failed.

Day 5 (no signer)

Pose extraction. Pilot re-run with the paired check. Webcam letter test. Record results on the experiments page.

Decide before Day 5

  • Pilot words: keep filming if the filmed words improve with the paired check REAL; stop if they don't.
  • Letters: the demo is usable for real signers if webcam top-1 is close to 78%; if it falls far below, retrain with the new poses before anything else.

Rules

  • Consent first, using the forms in the project's consent folder. Recordings are not published; only keypoints may be shared.
  • Every clip records: signer ID, word or sentence, take, camera, date, consent ID.
  • Never test on a signer the model was trained on.
