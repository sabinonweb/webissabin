Task board

Each task opens its tool. Progress fills in by itself from the work saved in this browser; set the status when a task is handed over or finished.

How the files move

  1. The team sends a clip list (.json) and a folder of clips, privately, never through this site.
  2. The checker opens the tool, picks the list and the folder, and answers. Work saves in the browser.
  3. The checker presses Download and sends the file back. The team grades it.

Why the videos are never online: the broadcast recordings and our signers' recordings are not ours to publish. Our data rules allow keeping body and hand points, not raw video. These pages are public; the videos stay on the computers of the people doing the work.
