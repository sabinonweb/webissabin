How to decide

Yes ✓

  • The clip contains this sign, even if other signs come before or after it.
  • A regional variant of the same word still counts as yes; add a note in the box.
  • Same meaning, different but standard form of the word (e.g. plural): yes.

No ✗

  • The sign in the clip is a different word.
  • There is signing, but not this word anywhere in the clip.
  • Type the word that was really signed if you know it: it becomes a new labelled example.

Not sure ?

  • Too fast, half cut off, or you do not know this sign.
  • Two signs look alike and you cannot tell which.
  • "Not sure" is a useful answer. Do not guess yes or no to avoid it.
