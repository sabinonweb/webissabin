How to decide

  • Signing ✓: hands are signing through more than about half the clip, even with several signs or no caption on screen.
  • Not signing ✗: hands still, talking head, title card, intro, or a different segment.
  • Decide in a few seconds. Don't skip hard ones: skipping biases the result.
