How to decide

Signing ✓

  • Hands are signing for more than about half of the clip.
  • Several signs in a row: still ✓.
  • Starts or ends a little early or late, but signing fills most of it: ✓.
  • Signing with no caption or word on screen: ✓. You judge the hands, not the text.

Not signing ✗

  • Hands down or still for most of the clip.
  • Mostly talking or explaining to camera without signing.
  • Title card, intro, logo, blank screen, or a different part of the video.
  • The signer is out of frame or the picture is unreadable.

Keep it honest

  • Decide in a few seconds. Over 100 clips a few close calls do not change the result.
  • Do not skip hard clips: skipping only the hard ones makes the number look better than it is.
  • You are not judging which word it is. That is the word check.
  • Example already decided: t000 is ✓.
