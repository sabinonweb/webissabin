How to decide

Correct ✓

  • The interpreter signs the word shown, inside this clip.
  • The sign is slightly blended with the next one: still correct.
  • Untick "inside the clip" if the sign is cut off at the start or end, even when the word is right.

Wrong ✗

  • A different sign, a gesture, or no signing.
  • A sign that only looks similar (same hand shape, different movement or place).
  • Type the real word if you know it: wrong hits with a correction are valuable too.

Not sure ?

  • You can see a sign but cannot tell which.
  • The interpreter box is too small or blurred to judge.
  • Only confirmed hits become training data, so "not sure" is always safe.
