Rules: what to mark and how

Who to watch

  • Only the sign language interpreter (often a small box in the corner). Ignore the news reader and the pictures.
  • If the interpreter box disappears, mark that stretch as rest / not signing and move on.

Where a sign starts and ends

  • Start [: the moment the hand takes the shape and begins the sign's own movement.
  • End ]: when that movement finishes, before the hand travels to the next sign.
  • Do not include the travel between signs. Gaps between marks are normal.
  • Use 0.5× speed and Shift+←/→ (0.2 s steps) to place marks.

What to write

  • The Nepali word for the sign, the way you would gloss it. Not the caption text, and not what the reader said.
  • Use the base form: सरकार, not सरकारले; जानु, not जानुभयो.
  • A fixed phrase signed as one unit (प्रतिनिधि सभा): one mark, and say so in the note.
  • The same sign repeated: one mark per repetition only if the hands clearly stop between them.

Choosing the type

  • sign: a lexical sign you recognise.
  • fingerspelled: a name or word spelled letter by letter. Type the whole word, e.g. पोखरा.
  • pointing / gesture: pointing at a person, place or the screen; a shrug; a gesture any hearing person would make.
  • rest / not signing: hands down or still for more than about a second. No word needed.

When you are not sure

  • Still mark it and untick I'm sure. An unsure mark is far more useful than a gap.
  • Recognise the meaning but not the exact word? Write your best word and add a note.
  • No idea at all? Type ?, untick sure. It tells us a sign is there.
  • A regional or unusual sign: write the word and note "variant".

A good session

  • Watch 20–30 seconds at 1× first, then go back and mark at 0.5×.
  • Work in 10–15 minute stretches. Everything saves as you go.
  • Finish one stretch completely before moving on: mark every sign in it, not just the easy ones.
  • At the end, press Download annotations and send the file to the team.

Parliament-specific

  • Formal phrases repeat constantly: माननीय सदस्य, सभामुख, प्रतिनिधि सभा, राष्ट्रिय सभा. Mark each, as one unit if signed as one.
  • Members' names are often fingerspelled or shown with a name sign: choose fingerspelled or note "name sign".
  • Long speeches: take 1–2 minute stretches from different sittings, not one speech end to end.
